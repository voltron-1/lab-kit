Sysmon 1 events chain into a tree: ParentProcessGuid points at the parent's ProcessGuid, so you can walk ancestry precisely — the endpoint version of a zeek uid.
The command line is the payload: -enc, -w hidden, and the parent image together tell you intent faster than any hash.
A document (WINWORD.EXE) spawning an interpreter (powershell.exe) is the shape that matters — read the ancestry, not just the process.
