## BRIEF
In this lab, you walk Sysmon Event 1 process trees using `ProcessGuid` and `ParentProcessGuid`.

## GUIDED STEPS

1. Inspect `files/sysmon.json` and `files/sysmon-fields.md`.
2. Fill out `answers.txt`:
   ```text
   q1=pg-ps1
   q2=winword.exe
   q3=cmd.exe
   q4=whoami /all
   q5=3
   q6=cm-0311-0201
   ```
3. Verify with `lab check soc L3.2`.
