Enrichment answers 'what is this indicator' from three angles: VT-style detections, WHOIS ownership/age, and passive DNS resolutions.
A detection ratio is a signal, not a verdict, and a fresh registration or single-domain IP is often more telling than the score.
The graded path is offline mocks by design — real WHOIS/dig/VT are optional, so no lab ever depends on a live service.
